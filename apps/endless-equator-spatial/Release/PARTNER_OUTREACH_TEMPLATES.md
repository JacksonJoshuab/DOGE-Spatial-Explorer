# Ecuador partner confirmation templates

These messages are ready for producer review and translation. Send from the approved production account and store responses in the private evidence system. Do not mark a partner confirmed based on a phone call alone; follow with written confirmation.

## Local guide and motorcycle fleet

**Subject:** September/October Ecuador enduro expedition — route verification and supported fleet request

Hello [Name],

We are preparing a small, professionally supported Ecuador motorcycle expedition for [exact dates]. The working route connects the Quito/Pifo area with Cayambe, Papallacta, Baeza, Archidona/Tena, Baños, Quilotoa/Chugchilán, the Cotopaxi perimeter, Machachi, and an optional Pacific extension. The current map is planning-only; we will not operate any unverified trail or protected-area segment.

Please confirm whether your team can provide:

- a named Ecuador-based route verifier and lead/sweep guides;
- inspection and an exact GPX file for legal public/authorized roads;
- current access, closure, landowner/community, and protected-area checks;
- [number] suitable enduro motorcycles, VIN/model list, tires/mousses, spares, tools, mechanic, recovery, and replacement terms;
- insurance scope, exclusions, deposits, damage terms, and roadside/recovery capability;
- support-vehicle access, bailout points, fuel, communications, and emergency planning;
- availability, itemized quote, taxes, cancellation terms, and payment schedule.

Please identify any segment you would decline or reroute and the authority/source supporting that decision. We will hash the final GPX and use it only with the agreed verification record.

Thank you,
[Producer name / company / phone / email]
