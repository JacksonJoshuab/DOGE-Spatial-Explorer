WebKit deployment

Safari and WebKit support.

The deployment is designed for current Safari on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, Add to Home Screen mode, and native WKWebView containers. JavaScript and local website storage must be enabled.

Recommended setup

  • Use a current operating-system release and close other demanding apps before a timed session.
  • Keep the device awake, disable Low Power Mode for formal timing review, and use a stable touch or pointer method.
  • Install from Safari with Add to Home Screen for an app-like, offline-capable shell.
  • For research or performance claims, validate timing on each supported device and browser class.

Known boundary

Browser and WKWebView timing is suitable for product evaluation and wellness tracking after device-class validation. It is not automatically equivalent to the native iOS timing evidence path.
